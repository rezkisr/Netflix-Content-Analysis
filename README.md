# 🎬 Netflix Content Analysis

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project that examines Netflix's content library using Python. This project explores content distribution, production countries, genres, audience ratings, and catalog growth to uncover key trends and insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Netflix's content library to uncover trends in content types, production countries, genres, audience ratings, and catalog growth. Using Python for data cleaning, analysis, and visualization, the project answers key business questions and communicates insights through clear visualizations.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

1. How many total titles are available in Netflix's content library?
2. What is the distribution of Movies and TV Shows on Netflix?
3. Which countries produce the most content on Netflix?
4. Among the top 10 content-producing countries, which has the highest proportion of TV Shows compared to Movies?
5. What are the most common genres on Netflix?
6. What are the most common audience ratings on Netflix?
7. How has Netflix's content library grown over time?

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Movies account for approximately **70%** of Netflix's content library, making them the dominant content type.
- The **United States** is the largest content-producing country, followed by **India** and the **United Kingdom**.
- While movies dominate in most countries, **South Korea** and **Japan** have relatively higher proportions of TV Shows.
- **International Movies** and **Dramas** are the most common genres in Netflix's catalog.
- Most Netflix titles are intended for **mature audiences**, indicating a strong focus on adult viewers.
- Netflix's content library expanded rapidly between **2015 and 2019**, before the number of newly added titles gradually declined.
